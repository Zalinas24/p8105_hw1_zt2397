Taking the mean of “random sample” works as expected since it is numeric
data, and taking the mean of “positive also works since it treats
categorical data as 0 or 1. However, character vector has no numeric
meaning as it is text, and for”food groups” it is stored internally.

``` r
as.numeric (positive)
```

    ##  [1] 1 1 1 1 0 1 1 0 0 1

``` r
as.numeric (character_vector)
```

    ## Warning: NAs introduced by coercion

    ##  [1] NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA NA

``` r
as.numeric (food_group)
```

    ##  [1] 1 1 3 3 2 2 2 2 3 2

Applying “as.numeric” converts all of the values to numeric values, such
as the fruits, vegetables, and protein are assigned 1,2, and 3. This
helps explain why mean fails on charcater vector as these values do not
represent any meaningful numbers, so averaging creates non-meaningful
results.
